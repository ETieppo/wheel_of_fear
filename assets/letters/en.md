# Wheel of Fear - Letters (English)

One section per sector. The game shows the text of section N in the letter of sector N.

## 1
Whenever I looked inward I felt a chill, the kind that made everything seem
artificial, as if everything around me could stop making sense at any moment.
But I never paid it any mind. Time tends to run against me, and you know the
worst part? It always wins...

## 2
When we started this project, we believed we were taking humanity to another
level, a place where all of us could know, could reach a life we would never
have dreamed of on our own. The machine we built, cough cough, or rather, the
machines, became so... untamable... that this is how it ended...

## 3
Now I find myself alone, with these... things... that we once called support
and tools. Who would have thought we would be the price. No way out, no
strength, and what is left to me? Fear? Strangely, that feels like something
good; maybe getting out of here is the reason. But I wonder why such hope. We
built them to be better, we just didn't know that... I... I have to go, there
is no more time.

## 4
In the beginning it seemed to be the way, the solution. We stopped "betting"
on social solutions, on rulers, and it didn't even make much of a difference,
probably because we were so few, and after the tragedy we lived through we
started taking better care of each other. But then we began to set aside
conscience, humanity, then health, until it reached education, and that was
when I realized how atrocious the thing we were creating was.

## 5
After everything, I came to admire the way you acted. The times I saw you
fail, you were trying. It was beautiful how you smiled at people, even when
embarrassed. I am so sorry, and I think I speak for everyone you must have
found along the way. We destroyed the most precious thing we had: being human.
It's funny how that sounds, "human being". It's a literal word, yet it should
give meaning to what we call ourselves and how we act. A pity it ends like
this.

## 6
The screams in the corridors are unbearable. I'm afraid I won't be able to
escape.

## 7
I remember when they began to replace the workforce, as if it were already
easy to get anything in a place so small. The scale changed after the end, but
the problems were the same, and after that the chill was no longer only there
when I looked at myself; it followed me wherever I went.

## 8
I can't stop, they are getting closer and closer. Something has to give
meaning to all of this, even if it is a single life!

## 9
I barely escaped the last one. They have reached the final stage; now they
improve on their own. They learned to hunt. Some of them no longer answer to
any light, others no longer seem to care about noise.

## 10
I wish the trauma would not follow you beyond the gate. I don't think the end
I'll have is enough to pay such a price.

## 11
Along the way you must have wondered why we chose you. If by now you still
don't know, you, like some of us, are a developer. You took part in building
it. Although others were part of our community too, you seemed to be against
all of this. Every now and then I caught myself watching you as you glimpsed
from afar how the generators worked. After a few years I noticed you began
scribbling in your notebook, thoughtfully, almost as if you were planning a way
to destroy all of this at once. If you have this letter, it's because you found
my body. I am bleeding and I don't believe I will survive. We failed as a
society. We were few, what was left after our last invention. Thousands of us
were wiped out. We made the same mistake: we thought building it would
accelerate the pillars. We were blind, and now we pay the price.

## 12
But in the end, the reason. It may seem like a punishment, but I knew you
would find a way: a way out, a way to live, and most importantly, a way to
destroy all of this, to correct our mistake and be able to live in peace. As
for us, none of us deserved such a gift. I tried to activate the generators
myself, but I saw there was no time for the overload. You were the last one
left from the escape pod; the others were destroyed and died in the process.
The last generator is linked to the others as a reverse current. The energy is
their life, if we can even call it that.
